About Best Cars

Home to the finest domestic and imported vehicles in North America — where quality meets a price you'll love.

Our Story

Founded in 1998, Best Cars has grown from a single showroom in Chicago into one of North America's most trusted dealership networks. For over two decades, our promise has stayed the same: every vehicle we sell is rigorously inspected, honestly priced, and backed by a team that treats customers like neighbors, not numbers.
Today we carry more than 40 premium brands — from rugged American trucks to refined European sedans and cutting-edge electric vehicles. Whether you're buying your first car or your fifth, our specialists guide you through trade-ins, financing, and after-sales care with zero pressure and full transparency.
